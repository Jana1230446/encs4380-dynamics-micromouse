Dynamic Error Calculator

Find the maximum time constant for a chosen frequency and allowed amplitude error.

The result is the largest time constant that keeps the output amplitude loss within your allowed error. A smaller τ means the system can follow faster signals more accurately. Calculated from M = 1/√(1 + (2πfτ)²), with M ≥ 1 − allowed error.
